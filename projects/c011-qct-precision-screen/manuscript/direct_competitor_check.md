# Direct-competitor and novelty check

**Status:** bounded check updated September 2026. This is not a systematic review and does not support an unqualified “first” claim.

## Search target

A direct competitor would evaluate the post-2016 HUD QCT relative-MoE screen as the policy object and report national coverage, designation churn, population-conditioned eligibility loss, or cap-mediated status changes under an equivalent same-data threshold contrast.

## Closest sources

| Source | What it establishes | Boundary relative to C011 |
|---|---|---|
| [HUD 2016 QCT notice](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2015-11-24/pdf/2015-29953.pdf) | Official rule change, approximately 50% MoE ratio, three ACS products, QCT statutory setting. | Governing policy source; no counterfactual impact evaluation. |
| [HUD QCT algorithm](https://www.huduser.gov/portal/qct/QCT_Algorithm.html) | Public operational structure for eligibility, ranking, and allocation. | Enables reconstruction; no population-gradient or churn analysis. |
| [Census guidance on overlapping ACS period estimates](https://www.census.gov/newsroom/blogs/random-samplings/2022/03/period-estimates-american-community-survey.html) | Consecutive five-year products share component years and require careful comparison. | Constrains accuracy claims; it is not a QCT impact analysis. |
| [Soltas, *Tax Incentives and the Supply of Low-Income Housing*](https://evansoltas.com/papers/SoltasJMP.pdf) | Reconstructs QCT assignment and uses ACS sampling variation in a housing-supply design. | Closest empirical neighbor. Its estimand is housing supply, not the precision screen's administrative coverage and cap propagation. |
| Treasury, *Strategies for Determining Eligibility of Homeowners Based on Income* | Notes that ACS sampling error varies with sample size and that MoE rules can affect lower-population areas. | Direct policy ancestor for the general concern, but not a QCT replay. |

## Defensible positioning

Use: “a nationwide deterministic 2016 policy-sensitivity analysis of HUD's QCT precision screen.”

Do not use: “the first,” “breakthrough,” “proves bias,” “natural experiment,” or “refutes administrative neutrality.” The present contribution is the exact 2016 reconstruction, the verified screen-threshold contrast, the five-part churn partition, and the prespecified population standardization.

## Evidence-state constraint

D037 controls manuscript admissibility. Only 2016 is independently regenerable from retained provenance. The project therefore cannot claim multi-year recurrence and remains parked as a first-paper project absent materially new authoritative operational provenance.
