# Real-World Statistical Reliability

This folder is the canonical research memory for a research program on **reliable statistical inference under imperfect real-world data**.

The project originated as **Real-World ML Reliability**. That name is retained in some historical records and as the current project/container lineage, but the scientific charter is now broader than machine learning. Per D015, infrastructure/repository renaming is deferred until a first execution project survives.

## Purpose

These records prevent repeated rediscovery, preserve why decisions were made, and give future human or AI collaborators a compact starting state before searching, analyzing, coding, or proposing projects.

## Current state

D039 is the controlling program decision.

> **C013 — release-provenance and temporal-interpretability audit of repeated CDC PLACES/500 Cities estimates — SURVIVES / CONTROLLED EXECUTION DESIGN.**

The candidate asks whether published temporal contrasts, trajectories, rankings, hotspot/set classifications and decision statements survive after nominal release time is replaced with documented BRFSS source time, deterministic carry-forwards are removed, definitions and geography/population rules are harmonized, and uncertainty limitations are respected.

The full prosecution verified nine eligible temporal-use works, completed a 2016–2025 measure/release provenance crosswalk, and reproduced three contrasting public-archive cases. It found exact copied waves in rotating mammography, high-cholesterol and colorectal-screening measures while preserving negative-control outcomes with genuinely distinct source years. No direct prior work was located that combines a temporal-use corpus, finalized provenance crosswalk, exact-copy diagnostics and conclusion-level sensitivity analysis.

The candidate does **not** estimate true local health trends, declare all repeated-release analyses invalid, or authorize named-paper error claims without exact reproduction. The authoritative evidence record is `22_C013_PLACES_TEMPORAL_PROSECUTION.md`; the next binding document is `23_C013_CONTROLLED_EXECUTION_PROTOCOL.md`.

**Authorized next action:** begin Stage 0 provenance archiving and machine-readable crosswalk construction under the frozen C013 protocol. Do not draft a manuscript or conference abstract until the controlled execution evidence gate passes.

## Historical C011 record

**C011 — Precision-screen reliability versus geographic coverage in HUD QCT designation**

**Status:** **PARKED / NO-GO FOR FIRST PAPER UNDER CURRENT EVIDENCE / REOPEN ONLY WITH MATERIALLY NEW AUTHORITATIVE OPERATIONAL PROVENANCE**

C011 emerged only after two null discovery passes and a three-channel theory/data/decision triangulation. Its promotion rests on an exact nationwide same-data policy replay rather than a literature-gap assertion. The current HUD precision screen materially changes eligibility and designation, the burden is population-dependent even after substantive-threshold conditioning, and the 20% cap creates nonlocal allocation changes.

The candidate's novelty is intentionally bounded: reconstruction of QCT assignment, generic ACS sampling-error instability and generic selective-classification disparities are prior art [R269–R270, R274, R278]. The surviving contribution is evaluation of the **post-2016 precision gate itself** as a selective reliability policy, with an exact distinction between identified policy effects and nonidentified latent accuracy.

Full empirical record: `18_CROSS_FIELD_STATISTICS_RECONNAISSANCE.md` D033. Formal dossier: `04_CANDIDATE_REGISTRY.md` C011.
